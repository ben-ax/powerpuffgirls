Safe and Easy Hiring

Housekeeping is the most important part of a hotel and the best place for beginners to start working.

  • Smart Task Sharing: Trainees do simple, repeating tasks. This gives the experienced staff more time to solve hard problems.
  • Easy to Start: You need to be exact and follow routines. You do not need perfect language skills right away.
  • Building Confidence: It is a safe place for beginners and immigrants to learn how to work in a hotel and practice the language.

The Career Ladder

A good system does not keep people in beginner jobs forever; it builds a ladder for them to climb. When trainees are good at housekeeping, they move to jobs where they meet the guests.

  • The Breakfast Team: A great place to learn teamwork and how to work fast. It gives the hotel motivated workers during the busy morning.
  • Reception: The face of the hotel. Grateful and happy trainees give guests a warm welcome, making the hotel experience better.
  • A Mixed Team: It creates a team with different backgrounds, which is perfect for welcoming international guests.

The Next Step

What happens when the training is over? A successful program makes sure trainees leave ready for the regular working world.

  • A Real CV: The hotel and OsloKollega help write a professional CV and give a verified work reference. This unlocks their future career.
  • The Next Job: The best trainees are offered permanent contracts at this hotel or other Strawberry hotels. Others find good jobs in new companies.
  • The Alumni Network: Successful graduates tell their friends to apply. This gives the hotel a free, endless line of motivated new workers.
Societal Impact: We help move people off welfare support and turn them into active, independent taxpayers.
